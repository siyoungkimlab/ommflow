ommflow documentation
=====================

``ommflow`` prepares and runs explicit-solvent protein molecular dynamics with
OpenMM. It reads hydrogen-complete PDB structures and structural DMS or MAE
files, applies a selected protein and water force field, solvates and
neutralizes the system, equilibrates it, and runs restartable production MD.
